## Korean — Naver Blogs

### rysso_ ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Naver blog
- **URL:** https://m.blog.naver.com/rysso_
- **Specialty:** Systematic bra construction taxonomy — cup types, seam types, wire families
- **Why valuable:** The cup seam series (221910351034) is the clearest single taxonomy of cup construction found in any language. Explicitly maps seam type to breast shape. Names shallow-bust indicators.
- **Best start:** https://m.blog.naver.com/rysso_/221910351034 (cup seams part 1)
- **Translated:** Yes — https://bra-research.netlify.app/articles/rysso-cup-seams-1.html

### ambitionner ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Naver blog
- **URL:** https://m.blog.naver.com/ambitionner
- **Specialty:** Bra fit diagnosis with photos; cup coverage vs volume; gore fit analysis
- **Why valuable:** Best Korean explainer of the coverage/volume distinction and open/closed upper cup edge. Includes fit-failure photos. Explicitly notes Korea produces almost only 3/4 cups.
- **Best start:** https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=ambitionner&logNo=220733164618
- **Translated:** Yes — https://bra-research.netlify.app/articles/ambitionner-full-34-12-cup.html

### 한선미 속옷 디자인 연구소 (lina7792) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Naver blog
- **URL:** https://m.blog.naver.com/lina7792
- **Specialty:** Professional custom bra drafting for Korean body types; runs a lingerie design institute
- **Why valuable:** Documents bespoke drafting workflow treating BP height and lateral cup circumference as first-class inputs — not just band+cup size. Case study is 70B (our target range). Goal: build a Korean-women-specific size/shape database because imported patterns fail.
- **Best start:** Custom bra drafting case series (맞춤형 브라 실무패턴 제도)
- **Especially relevant to:** Underbust 65–70, outer cup fit issues, Korean body-type drafting

### beautefit ⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Naver blog
- **Specialty:** Korean lingerie patternmaking vocational course posts; wire drafting principles, molded cup factory visits
- **Why valuable:** Professional training context; wire-first drafting method documented

### sewingladystudio 쏘잉레이디 ⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** YouTube (Korean)
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@sewingladystudio (4.84K subs)

- **Best video:** Easy Bra Draping 브라드레이핑 — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PK1G3tIqrLk
- **Why valuable:** One of the only Korean YouTube channels doing actual bra pattern work; almost everything else is sizing guides

### 옷짱 ⭐⭐⭐

- **Specialty:** Underwear block drafting from body measurements (어르신 속옷 패턴 그리기 — 23K views)

## Japanese — Blogs & Sites

### armeria-marine ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Personal blog (professional garment patternmaker / 3D CAD specialist)
- **URL:** https://www.armeria-marine.com
- **Specialty:** 3D CAD bra pattern making; cup shape taxonomy; failure analysis of standard dress forms
- **Why valuable:** Best available explanation of WHY flat drafting fails for shallow busts (industrial ease removes the apex-to-underbust break). Gold standard = plaster casting. Cup taxonomy: bowl / Fuji / plate shapes.
- **Best start:** https://www.armeria-marine.com/2017/04/01/ブラジャーについての考察/
- **Translated:** Yes — https://bra-research.netlify.app/articles/armeria-marine-bra-considerations.html

### HEAVEN Japan ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** note.com + YouTube
- **Specialty:** 45-year professional bra patternmaker; industry-level content
- **Why valuable:** Rare: a career bra patternmaker posting content publicly. Note posts are marketing-adjacent but YouTube/pattern content is professional grade.
- **Search:** "HEAVEN Japan ブラ" on YouTube; https://note.com (search HEAVEN Japan)

### intimate jimura ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Online notions shop — https://www.jimura.jp
- **Specialty:** Bra-making notions with published dimension charts; pattern kits (パターン&レシピ) at specific sizes
- **Why valuable:** Only hobbyist-accessible source publishing h×w×l tables for multiple wire shapes (U/C/L) at small sizes (underbust 60–70). L-type wires are ~1.1cm narrower and ~2cm shorter at the same size — narrow-root geometry unavailable in Western catalogs.
- **Wire page:** https://www.jimura.jp/product-list/38
- **Wire dimensions:** U-type ABC65 = 10.5h × 6.4w × 18.7l cm; L-type ABC65 = 10.8h × 5.3w × 16.6l cm

### うさこの洋裁工房 (Yousai Koubou) ⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Website — https://yousai.net
- **Specialty:** Free pattern drafting guides; includes bikini/bra drafting with size adjustment instructions
- **Why valuable:** Free Japanese-language drafting, small-size coverage

### Sewing Atelier Menuet ⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Blog — https://menuet-dance.com
- **Specialty:** Non-wire bra drafting; draping from existing bra; かこみ製図 pipeline
- **Why valuable:** Documents the full drape→pattern→sew chain for a small soft bra; draping on existing pad-in cup = auto-corrects to shallow shape
- **Best start:** https://menuet-dance.com/relax-bra/

### YouTube playlist — 下着を作ってみよう！ ⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLvwQND68MGhGny_ET2XycZ7i4WeMFafWq
- **Specialty:** Japanese bra/underwear sewing series with drafting content

### ameblo weathersf ⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Ameba blog
- **Specialty:** One of few JP hobbyist wired-bra builds; uses Western pattern (Maya Bra) — documents the import workaround
- **Why valuable:** Evidence for scarcity hypothesis; confirms JP home sewing imports Western wired patterns

## Chinese — Academic & Bilibili

### 亚太服装学校 ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Bilibili (1,023 followers)
- **Specialty:** Vocational bra patternmaking lectures; wire-first drafting (钢圈基本型 + 罩杯基本型)
- **Best video:** BV1ib4y1i7Cj (13:29 min; wire + cup basic block)
- **URL:** https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1ib4y1i7Cj/
- **Why valuable:** Real classroom drafting confirming wire-first cup construction — the Chinese industry default

### 天衣E恋 (Zhihu) ⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Zhihu column
- **Specialty:** Bra component anatomy; gore height guidance by bust size
- **Why valuable:** Explicitly recommends low gore (低鸡心) for small busts/flat ribs; part-by-part anatomy dictionary
- **Best article:** https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/34795006

### Hans Publishers (academic) ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Platform:** Open-access Chinese academic journal
- **Specialty:** 3D virtual fitting + pattern correction with actual numbers
- **Paper:** 基于三维虚拟建模的文胸结构优化设计 — https://www.hanspub.org/journal/paperinformation?paperid=66067
- **Why valuable:** Quantitative correction vocabulary (BPE segment extended 7→8.5cm to fix low gore/empty cup); full bibliography of Chinese bra-drafting academic literature

## English — Useful Cross-Reference

These English sources appear in Asian materials and bridge the ecosystems:

- **Beverly Johnson / Pinup Girls Patterns** — Canadian designer referenced by Korean blogs as the Western bra-pattern benchmark. Patterns: Classic, Shelley (diagonal seam), Sweet Sixteen (good for shallow busts). https://www.pinupgirlspatterns.com
- **Sparkly Sewist** — Simple bralette draping tutorial; same workflow the JP/ZH sources formalize — https://sparklysewist.com/how-to-drape-a-bralette-pattern/
- **Bunka Fashion Graduate University (Dr. Ito Yumiko)** — Plaster-bandage + washi paper body→pattern method. Patented 2011. https://dricomeye.net/02_kenkyu/kenkyu_140120.html
