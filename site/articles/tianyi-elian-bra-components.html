Anatomy of a Bra: Every Component Explained

From the 天衣E恋 (Tianyi E-Lian) Zhihu column 「小天说内衣」, 内衣基本构件大科普, edited 2018-03-21. Translated from Chinese. Companion article: structure & classification of the bra (黎兜兜, 2015).

Translator's note on gore height. This article states outright that women with small or flat busts (贫乳, pínrǔ) — or with a protruding ribcage — should choose a low gore (低鸡心, dī jīxīn), while high gores suit fuller busts. This is the opposite of typical Western retail guidance, which treats a tall, firmly anchored gore as the default for everyone. For small-bust sewers this matters a lot: a tall gore on a shallow bust cannot make wire-to-chest contact, so it floats, presses on breast tissue or the sternum, and drags the whole bridge of the bra off the ribcage. A low gore lets a shallow cup sit flat against a shallow chest. When drafting or altering a small-bust pattern, treat gore height as a fit variable — not a fixed constant.

The Parts of a Bra

The Parts of a Bra (内衣零件篇)

Cup surface — 罩杯面 (zhào bēi miàn)

In cup construction, a change of a single detail determines whether a bra has real support or not. The cup surface plays the key role in supporting the breasts and can prevent premature sagging.

Gore — 鸡心位 / 脊心 (jī xīn wèi / jǐ xīn)

The gore (literally "chicken heart") is the piece connecting the two cups between the underwires. It controls the distance between the cups, joins them, and keeps them from shifting. Girls with small or flat busts (贫乳), or with a protruding ribcage, are better off with a low gore (低鸡心); a high gore gives better support and suits fuller-busted women.

Underwire — 钢圈 (gāng quān)

The stiffest ring along the lower edge of the cup. Its main jobs are maintaining the shape of the breast and providing support. Usually metal, sometimes plastic. Full-busted women are advised to choose firmer wires.

Straps — 肩带 (jiān dài)

Wider straps give better lifting force; narrower straps are more comfortable on the shoulders. Fuller busts benefit from wide straps; smaller busts can choose thinner straps for comfort.

Strap attachment point — 耳仔位 / 耳位 (ěr zǎi wèi)

Where the cup connects to the strap, transferring the strap's lift into the cup. The ideal position is directly above the nipple, where upward pull works best.

Side wing — 围带 / 侧比 (cè bǐ)

The taller the side wing, the more stable the bra. For a wide-set (外扩) or full bust, choose a side wing 6–8 cm tall — it covers more of the underarm area. In other cases, 4–5 cm is enough.

Under-cradle band — 底围 (dǐ wéi)

The band beneath the cups. Different bras have different band widths; the wider the band, the wider the matching side wing.

Hook-and-eye closure — 内衣扣 (nèiyī kòu)

Its main function is keeping the bra fitted close to the body. The soft fleece backing of the closure rows can also be used to clean bras.

Sizing (码数篇)

Step 1 — Measure. The underbust (下围): ribcage circumference directly under the breast crease — the "75" in 75A. The overbust (上围): measured across the shoulder blades to the nipples, the fullest circumference including the breasts.

Step 2 — Calculate. Cup size: overbust minus underbust = cup letter (A, B, C, D…).

Step 3 — Look up your size. This is only a rough guide — many women find bras chosen this way uncomfortable because breasts also differ in shape and direction of growth.

Breast Shapes (胸型篇)

Judging shape is mostly about the base (底盘 — the footprint on the chest) versus the projection (隆起).

  • Plate/disc shape (圆盘型): large base, low projection, flat. → Suits the airy triangle cup (三角杯).
  • Conical (圆锥型): medium base, medium projection, upright. → Suits 3/4 cups and Japanese-style bras.
  • Hemispherical (半球型): medium base, high projection, full. → Avoid thick padded or compressive bras. Prefer a full-cup bra with firm wires.
  • Spindle shape (纺锤型): small base, high projection, heavy with sagging tendency. → Priority: anti-sag. Need full cups and wide side wings.

Sports Bras (运动篇)

Sports bras are designed with better shock absorption and fixation, reducing the burden soft breasts place on the body during movement. Always wear one when exercising.

How to Put On a Bra (穿戴篇)

Hang the straps on your shoulders; facing the center point, gather the breasts from front to back and fasten the closure. Reach into the cups and gently sweep the side tissue (副乳, armpit-adjacent breast tissue) forward into the cups. Straighten up, adjust strap length, check that the underband is level all the way around. Finally, nudge the side bands slightly downward to lift the whole bust line.

Washing (清洗篇)

Hand-washing recommended; if machine-washing, use a dedicated laundry bag. No hot water — it damages fibers. Rinse thoroughly to prevent residue build-up and yellowing.

Companion: The Structure and Classification of the Bra

By 黎兜兜 (Li Doudou), 「兜兜的内衣课堂」 — 文胸的结构，以及分类, edited 2015-09-30. Summarised translation of the component-naming article.

1. Basic structure

A bra is generally built from straps (肩带), cups (罩杯), back wing (后拉片, also 肶位/侧翼), gore (鸡心/心位), wire casing (钩圈), and hook-and-eye (钩扣).

Note on gore height: Gore level with the cup apex (峰点) = high gore; below it = low gore. High gores generally mean deeper cups or taller upper cups; low gores generally mean shallower cups.

1. Straps. Most bras have sliders for adjustment. Straps should be elastic but not too elastic — they help the cups lift and add stability. Single-piece sliders sit at the back: wherever your body shape, the slider never ends up on the shoulder.

2. Cups. Contain and support the breasts, shaping the bust line. By material: moulded cups (模杯), lined/padded cups (夹棉), single-layer cups (单层). By coverage: full cup (全罩杯), 3/4 cup, 1/2 cup. By interior: padded (padding in lower/side-lower cup to add volume) vs. unpadded.

3. Back wing (后拉片). The main force-applying part, made of stretch fabric. By rear silhouette: straight wing (一字肶) and U-shaped wing (U形肶). Straight wings suit slim figures; U wings suit fuller ones. On a full bust in a straight wing, breast weight drags the straps down and hikes the back wing up.

4. Gore (鸡心). The small trapezoid joining the two cups. Forms: high gore, low gore, continuous gore, with cradle (有下扒) or without (无下扒). Gore height scales with cup volume: high gores = deeper cups; low gores = shallower cups.

5. Other parts. Underwire (钢圈): the cup is shaped around the wire; wire shape is derived from the body — breast height, spacing, curve. Hook-and-eye: back-closure bras hold shape best (2–3 adjustment rows); front-closure bras show more cleavage but hold shape worse and suit smaller busts only; side-closure bras hold shape well and suit side-strap styles.

2. Specialty bras

  • Sports bras: cotton-Lycra and high-stretch knits, single-layer, usually wire-free; straps cut continuous with front and back pieces.
  • Nursing bras: double-layered with clasps at the upper or middle cup for feeding access. Breasts change most during nursing; good support reduces post-nursing deformation.

Translator's term notes: 贫乳 (pínrǔ) — small or flat bust; 鸡心 / 低鸡心 / 高鸡心 — gore / low gore / high gore; 副乳 (fù rǔ) — "auxiliary breast," the soft tissue near the armpit that can migrate into the cup; 底盘 (dǐpán) — breast root / base footprint on the chest; 圆盘型 — plate/disc shape (wide base, low projection) = the shallow bust archetype this research targets. Why we archived it: the explicit low-gore recommendation for small/flat busts (贫乳) and the plate/disc shape taxonomy (圆盘型 → triangle cup) are the clearest Chinese-language articulation of how small-bust geometry should drive bra selection — a default assumption absent from Western retail guidance.
