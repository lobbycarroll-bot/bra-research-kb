Bra Pattern Design and the Production Process

By 沉默-霸主 ("Silent Overlord"), Sina Blog. Translated from Chinese. Original: blog.sina.com.cn/s/blog_4400165f010127m7.html

Why this matters: a rare complete professional walkthrough of the Chinese industry workflow — from analyzing the sample garment through net-pattern drafting, grading (放码), and marker making. Its central insight: the cups are drafted from the underwire, not from body measurements (根据钢圈配图的基本原理). The underwire defines the cup bottom; everything else is built around it. References the core Chinese textbook 《内衣纸样设计原理与实例》 throughout.

I. Analyze the Sample Garment and Organize the Spec Sheet (制单, zhìdān)

From the style sketch and construction analysis, organize the basic data to prepare for pattern drafting and fabric estimation. This style has 11 pattern pieces. The construction sequence is derived from analyzing the sample garment.

II. Pattern Making (纸样制作)

1. Design approach

(1) The cups (罩杯, zhàobēi). First, draft the net pattern (净样, jìngyàng — without seam allowances) using the fundamental principle of drafting from the underwire (钢圈, gāngquān). Then adjust according to customer measurements. From the basic net pattern, derive patterns for the outer cup (面杯), lining/padding pocket pieces (里垫), and pocket fabric (袋布).

(2) The cradle and back wings (下扒与后片). Draft the cradle from the underwire shape. Most critical: the shirring allowance between the lower band (下围) and upper band (上围) — set at 0.5 cm per 10 cm.

2. Net-pattern drafting (净样制作)

(1) Drafting from the underwire. See 《内衣纸样设计原理与实例》 for full steps. Key relationship: cup bottom (杯底) = wire length + 1.5 cm wire-channel allowance (钢圈空位). The cup-bottom curve must match the wire's shape exactly.

(2) Splitting the lower cup. Adjust the cup-seam curve (杯骨, bēigǔ — "cup bone," the internal seam line) and cup-bottom curve so both run smoothly. Cup dart (罩杯省) kept at 1.5–2.5 cm.

(3) Straightening the outer-cup top edge. The top edge carries lace and must be straight. Pivot upper and lower cups around the BP point until cup-seam lines align, bisect the resulting dart, then open along the bisector until the top edge straightens.

(4) Padding and pocket fabric. Pocket opening: about 4.5 cm. Padding (棉垫): take the pocket-fabric pattern as base and drop the cup-seam line (杯骨) parallel by 1.5–2 cm.

(5) Cradle and back wings. Watch shirring allowance on lower and upper band edges. Center front may be curved.

3. Working patterns (毛样制作 — net patterns + seam allowances)

Add seam allowances and annotate all pattern pieces. Mark notches (剪口, jiǎnkǒu) wherever alignment points are needed.

4. Full size set (齐码纸样 — graded patterns)

Underwire grading: use the BP point as coordinate origin.

Cup grading: consider top edge, cup height, and cup bottom together; use multiple coordinate origins. Outer and inner cups must have identical grade increments at top edge, cup seam, cup bottom, and strap tab.

Cradle grading: by sequential extension (顺延放缩); grade increment at cup bottom = 1.3 cm. Back wings graded using grainline as coordinate reference.

IV. Correcting Pattern Defects (纸样弊端修正)

1. Adjusting the cup-top-edge size: (a) Shift the strap-tab point forward/backward — suits straps too far apart or too close. (b) Open/add along upper-cup height — suits a top edge pressing into chest (open) or too loose (overlap).

2. Adjusting cup-seam size: open/add along cup height to change top-edge size; cup width and dart amount change. Suits cup too big (overlap) or too small (open).

3. Adjusting the strap tab: adjust at top edge (changes style only, dart unchanged) or at cup seam (directly changes dart and seam size).

4. Cup shape and cup-bottom curve: the curve must match the wire — straight at front, curved at back. More projected front edge = straighter front curve; better side push-up = greater rear curvature.

5. "Eye-bag" wrinkle at side-support cup bottom: normal. To eliminate: increase curvature to match wire — but this causes looseness at the upper-band/strap junction, compensated by increasing construction shrinkage (工艺回缩量) there.

V. Extending the Bra Pattern

"Blade-head" cup variation (刀头罩杯 — a pointed/angled cup-panel construction); multi-panel structure variations.

Translator's term notes: 钢圈 (gāngquān) — underwire; the entire Chinese drafting system starts from its arc length and shape. 杯底 (bēidǐ) — cup bottom, the lower edge that sits in the wire channel; drafted as wire length + 1.5 cm channel allowance. 杯骨 (bēigǔ) — "cup bone," the internal cup seam/curve line. 罩杯省 (zhàobēi shěng) — cup dart; kept small at 1.5–2.5 cm. 净样 / 毛样 — net pattern (no seam allowances) / working pattern (with allowances added). 放码 / 档差 — grading / grade increment. 排料 — marker making (nesting patterns for fabric efficiency).
