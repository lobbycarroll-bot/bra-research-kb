About

English-language bra-making communities skew toward large-bust fitting problems. This collection translates and archives technical material from Asian-language sources — pattern drafting, cup/wire geometry, small-bust and shallow-bust fitting, draping, and corsetry — where the typical client's body is much closer to a small frame.

Translations are for personal study. Each article links its original source; all rights remain with the original authors.
