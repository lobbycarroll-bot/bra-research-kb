Order: JB3570 · L · BE (confirm live stock)

Size L is the only grade containing A80; S is only A65/B65. This is a flexible one-piece wireless moulded cup with lift sheet, high sides and twin-strap lift. How to place the Japanese order

Recommended: one active purchasing proxy

  1. Create a ZenMarket account.
  2. Paste each exact product URL into the proxy cart; repeat SKU, size and color in the notes.
  3. Wait for confirmed price and availability before funding.
  4. Add the Japanese sheet music and any wire samples.
  5. Wait until every item reaches the warehouse, then create one consolidated parcel.

ZenMarket's published ordinary-shop fee is generally ¥500 per item, initial consolidation is included, and storage is free for 60 days. Domestic Japanese shipping, payment fees, international shipping and U.S. import charges are additional.

Why not use only a forwarding address?

Wacoal and Nissen do not ship overseas, and Nissen asks customers not to designate forwarding companies as delivery addresses. An active buying proxy can confirm whether it can purchase from the merchant before taking payment.

WorldShopping: easy, but poor for uncertain fit

Where its cart appears, WorldShopping is convenient, but it currently charges a 10% purchasing fee plus international freight and a ¥500 package-handling fee. More importantly, it explicitly refuses returns or exchanges because the size did not fit, regardless of the retailer's domestic return policy. Use it only for a settled size or a garment worth keeping as a teardown.

U.S. tariffs and declared origin

A proxy solves access, not tariffs. U.S. duty-free de minimis treatment for low-value commercial shipments was suspended globally in August 2025 and continued in 2026. A Japanese warehouse does not make a product Japanese-origin: BMM022 and the Nissen bra are currently listed as made in China, while JB3570 is listed as made in Vietnam.
  • Ask which carriers currently accept U.S. apparel parcels and whether charges are prepaid (DDP) or collected on delivery (DDU).
  • Keep the itemized invoice and correct manufacturing origin for each garment.
  • Do not request gift marking or undervaluation.
  • Get the final packed shipping quote before authorizing export and keep a customs reserve.
  • Many man-made-fiber bras have historically carried a 16.9% general duty under HTSUS 6212.10.9020; classification and additional country measures can change, so this is not a final landed-cost estimate.

Home-construction research

Start from Jum's redrafted cradle and cup, not a stock pattern. Overlay the original Mésange and redraft; record the free and sprung France-36 curves; measure how far the apex moved and how much volume was removed. Preserve a minimal natural cup as the control, then copy selected retail mechanisms—padding maps, side slings, lift sheets and strap vectors—onto the same corrected cradle one variable at a time.

Before washing or cutting a purchased bra

Photograph the garment and record relaxed/stretched band, actual wire trace and spring, tip heights, gore dimensions, cup-shell perimeter, horizontal and vertical cup depth, apex position, pad thickness on a grid, pad-pocket and sling shapes, strap vectors, elastic tension and layer order. Complete the documented try-on with tags attached first.
