One consolidated Japanese research order

Optimize for the household's time: one information-dense purchasing, shipping and fitting turn—not the lowest item price. Fit credibility still comes first: exclude fashion-led, broad alpha-sized bras, but buy a deliberate size bracket when it can prevent another international order.

Priority 1Trust-first fit trial

Nissen Petitshu — ピタッと密着 / close-contact, no-gaping type

Order two: CJY0222D0001 · AAA80 and AA80 · same color if practical

In Nissen's three-style comparison, choose the center bra labelled ピタッと密着 (“close contact”), not the left natural-round or right fluffy-round design. This full-coverage wireless construction uses a non-removable stabilizing pad along the upper cup edge to reduce floating/gaping, a powernet lift panel at the lower cup, a wide underband and high sides. It is the clearest first test of whether an Asian-market AAA80 can remain in contact with Jum's very low-volume upper bust while adding controlled shape.

AAA80 is the lead fit hypothesis; AA80 is the controlled hedge. Holding band, model and color constant makes the comparison useful and gives this round a better chance of yielding one confidently wearable bra without waiting for a second Japanese order.

A current alternate listing is ZOZOTOWN item 95300390, which exposes Buyee and has shown AAA80 stock. Verify the Nissen model number CJY0222D0001 before payment.

How to place the Japanese order

Recommended: one active purchasing proxy

  1. Create a ZenMarket account.
  2. Submit the made-to-order BPP330 first, then submit both sizes of the close-contact Nissen, the Nissen soft-wire control and BMM022 immediately; repeat SKU, size and color in every note.
  3. Wait for confirmed price and availability before funding.
  4. Add all Japanese sheet music, wire samples and other already-planned Japanese research materials during the BPP330 production window.
  5. Tell the proxy not to form or export a parcel until BPP330 and every other item are in the warehouse. Confirm the 60-day storage clock leaves enough margin for its stated 1–1.5-month lead time.
  6. Create one consolidated international parcel.

ZenMarket's published ordinary-shop fee is generally ¥500 per item, initial consolidation is included, and storage is free for 60 days. Domestic Japanese shipping, payment fees, international shipping and U.S. import charges are additional. Those charges are constraints to budget for, not the optimization target; the goal is to minimize your coordination, waiting and refitting cycles.

Why not use only a forwarding address?

Wacoal and Nissen do not ship overseas, and Nissen asks customers not to designate forwarding companies as delivery addresses. An active buying proxy can confirm whether it can purchase from the merchant before taking payment.

WorldShopping: easy, but poor for uncertain fit

Where its cart appears, WorldShopping is convenient, but it currently charges a 10% purchasing fee plus international freight and a ¥500 package-handling fee. More importantly, it explicitly refuses returns or exchanges because the size did not fit, regardless of the retailer's domestic return policy. Use it only for a settled size or a garment worth keeping as a teardown.

U.S. tariffs and declared origin

A proxy solves access, not tariffs. U.S. duty-free de minimis treatment for low-value commercial shipments was suspended globally in August 2025 and continued in 2026. A Japanese warehouse does not make a product Japanese-origin: BMM022 and both Nissen bras are currently listed as made in China; BPP330 is listed as made in Japan.
  • Ask which carriers currently accept U.S. apparel parcels and whether charges are prepaid (DDP) or collected on delivery (DDU).
  • Keep the itemized invoice and correct manufacturing origin for each garment.
  • Do not request gift marking or undervaluation.
  • Get the final packed shipping quote before authorizing export and keep a customs reserve.
  • Many man-made-fiber bras have historically carried a 16.9% general duty under HTSUS 6212.10.9020; classification and additional country measures can change, so this is not a final landed-cost estimate.

Home-construction research

Start from Jum's redrafted cradle and cup, not a stock pattern. Overlay the original Mésange and redraft; record the free and sprung France-36 curves; measure how far the apex moved and how much volume was removed. Preserve a minimal natural cup as the control, then copy selected retail mechanisms—padding maps, side slings, lift sheets and strap vectors—onto the same corrected cradle one variable at a time.

Before washing or cutting a purchased bra

Photograph the garment and record relaxed/stretched band, actual wire trace and spring, tip heights, gore dimensions, cup-shell perimeter, horizontal and vertical cup depth, apex position, pad thickness on a grid, pad-pocket and sling shapes, strap vectors, elastic tension and layer order. Complete the documented try-on with tags attached first.
