Evidence that changed the test protocol

Fit must survive movement. A Taiwanese consumer with a 5 cm bust difference reported that rigid, thick “small-bust” bras looked fitted after scoop-and-swoop but shifted and gaped after ordinary movement. Other native accounts repeat the pattern. Every trial now includes raising arms, reaching, bending, twisting, walking, and re-photographing without adjustment.

We are testing edge contact, shallow curvature, band and strap adjustability, and padding placement—not just cleavage in one static pose. A bra can contain slight internal air yet still be visually and dynamically successful; a hard empty dome is a failure.

Score before returning or cutting

Photograph front, 3/4, side, and top before and after five minutes of movement. Score band stability, wire/root match, upper-edge contact, displacement, apex height, straps, comfort, and silhouette under a thin knit. For retained specimens, record wire trace/spring, cup perimeter and depth, gore, apex, padding thickness map, sling and pocket shapes, seam lines, stretch directions, and layer order. Copy promising wearables non-destructively before teardown.
