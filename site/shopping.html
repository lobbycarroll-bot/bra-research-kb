Jum's Bra Research Shopping List

Attractive fit trials + construction specimens · updated September 6, 2026

Current baseline: 78.0 cm snug underbust · 81.5 cm standing/apex bust · 3.5 cm difference · known wearable Pepper size S (BRA036/RN 174794) · Emerald Erin France wire 36 is the closest physical wire found. France 36 is a wire number, not a band size.
Do not convert the letter by itself. Jum's 78 cm underbust falls in Japanese band 80 on direct-measurement tables, but Japanese A usually assumes about a 10 cm bust difference. Her 3.5 cm difference is below that. In U.S. petite-brand systems her observed fit remains around 34AA–34A, with 34AAA worth testing where offered. Brand charts and real fit outrank generic country-conversion tables.

The market problem this cart is testing

The working hypothesis is an overlap failure: fashionable U.S. lingerie often stops before Jum's cup volume or scales cups from larger bodies; Asian catalogs contain more useful small-bust engineering, but language, inconsistent size systems and often conservative/high-coverage styling make the right products difficult to find. The cart therefore combines attractive Western micro-cup specialists with a small number of technically distinctive Asian specimens.

How to place the proxy order

  1. Create one ZenMarket account and submit BPP330 first.
  2. Submit both Nissen models, BMM022 and the two aimerfeel models; repeat SKU, size and color in every note.
  3. Add Japanese sheet music and any wire/material samples already planned.
  4. Do not form the international parcel until every item is in the warehouse. Confirm the storage clock leaves enough margin for BPP330's lead time.
  5. Export one parcel with an itemized invoice and correct manufacturing origins. A proxy solves access, not U.S. tariffs.

WorldShopping is easier where its cart appears but refuses fit-based returns. A simple forwarding address is also risky because some retailers reject forwarders. The active proxy is the time-first choice for this mixed-store order.

Explicitly removed or skipped

  • All Liberty recommendations: removed. The available grade is too imprecise; a cute non-fit would reduce confidence in the project.
  • Une Nana Cool B65 styles: wrong band after the 78 cm underbust measurement; do not revive the earlier 65A/B hypothesis.
  • Generic B/C fashion bras: no matter how attractive, they do not earn a research purchase unless a distinctive construction justifies a teardown.
  • Another stock Mésange: Jum already proved the stock cup was too large, its apex too high and its cradle/channel geometry unsuitable. Her redraft is the home-construction baseline.

Try-on and teardown protocol

Fit-test every returnable bra before washing or cutting. Photograph front, 3/4, side and top views; score band stability, wire/root match, upper-edge contact, apex height, strap position, movement and comfort. Then record relaxed/stretched band, wire trace and spring, gore, cup perimeter/depth, apex position, pad-thickness map, sling/pocket shapes, strap vectors, stretch directions and layer order. Return bad-fit duplicates; preserve the best natural fit, best controllable augmentation and best traceable cut-and-sew specimen.
