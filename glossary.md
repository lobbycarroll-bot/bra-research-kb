## Key Concepts with No Clean Single-Word Translation

**가로심/대각선심/세로심 (KO)** — The three seam-orientation families in Korean bra construction taxonomy: horizontal, diagonal, vertical. Each produces different projection and suits different breast shapes.

**컵 상변 개폐 (KO)** — "Open or closed upper cup edge": whether the top edge of the cup, viewed from the side, angles inward (closed = cups toward the upper chest) or outward (open = releases from upper chest). More predictive of fit than the coverage label (full/¾/½).

**かこみ製図 (JP)** — Literally "enclosed drafting." In bra making, specifically: tracing the shape from an existing bra or garment laid flat. The quickest way to get a working pattern from a bra that fits.

**鸡心 (ZH)** — Gore. The center bridge piece between the two cups. Height selection matters: Chinese sources recommend low gore (低鸡心) for small/flat busts, high gore for larger/wider-set busts — a different default than Western retail guidance.

**石膏包帯法 (JP)** — Plaster-bandage body casting. The academic gold standard for capturing the exact transition between bust apex and underbust — the critical curve that digital scanning and dress forms both smooth away.

**お皿型 (JP)** — "Plate/dish" cup shape. Nearly flat, designed for shallow bust tissue spread over a wide base. Explicitly recommended for small busts up to B–C. Almost absent from Western retail. Exemplar: Wacoal ウンナナクール.
