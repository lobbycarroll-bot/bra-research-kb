# Asian vs Western Bra-Making Defaults: Hypothesis and Evidence

## The Hypothesis
Asian bra makers may have accumulated different default drafting and fitting assumptions because their common client/body-size distribution differs from English-language hobby communities. English-language bra-making communities disproportionately focus on large-cup fitting problems.

**Status: Substantially supported, with nuance.**

---

## Evidence FOR Different Defaults

### Market and product defaults
- **Korea produces and retails almost exclusively 3/4-cup bras.** Ambitionner explicitly states this as a market fact, not a preference. Full cups and half cups are minority products in Korea.
- **Western market defaults to the opposite:** large-cup full-coverage bras dominate fitting discussions; 3/4 cup is taken as standard.
- **Chinese guidance on gore height:** Low gore (低鸡心) is the explicit default recommendation for small/flat busts. Western retail guidance treats gore height as a style choice, not a body-shape variable.

### Drafting method differences
- **Wire-first drafting is the Chinese/Japanese industry default.** Both Chinese vocational lectures (亚太服装学校 Bilibili) and the Chinese academic literature frame the wire arc as the first step: "learning bra patternmaking starts with drawing the underwire basic block." The cup is then built from the wire.
- **Western hobbyist patterns (Pinup Girls, etc.) start from body measurements.** The wire is sized afterward.
- **Implication for small frames:** Wire-first drafting inherently anchors the draft to the actual wire geometry, which scales correctly to small sizes. Measurement-first drafting may accumulate proportional errors at the small end.

### Wire supply and dimensions
- Western wire catalogs offer one geometry per size; no narrow-root variants listed.
- Japanese suppliers (jimura) publish multiple distinct shapes (U/C/L type) with tabulated h×w×l dimensions at small sizes. The L-type wire — designed for narrow-root applications — is ~1.1cm narrower and ~2.1cm shorter at size 65 than the U-type. This wire exists and is sold, but it is absent from Western suppliers' offerings.
- Western supplier language ("made to our exact specifications," no origin) suggests OEM manufacture from Chinese factories, but they only order the Western-market geometry.

### Academic and professional research
- Korean custom-pattern institute (한선미 연구소) **explicitly cites imported pattern failure** as the motivation for building a Korean-specific body database. Pattern failure symptoms: red wire marks at outer cup edge — wire extending past the breast root.
- Chinese academic work (Donghua University, 纺织学报) uses 3D Chinese anthropometric data to build localised bra blocks. They don't just apply Western block formulas; they re-derive the block from actual body scan data.
- Japanese patternmaker (armeria-marine) uses a 3D scan of a Japanese body **wearing a bra** (E cup) because CAD industrial bodies inadequately represent the apex-to-underbust transition. This is a workaround for an East-specific mismatch with Western industrial standards.

### Size distribution
- Documented drafting cases in Korean and Chinese technical sources cluster at underbust 65–75 / A–B cup — the range directly relevant to Jum.
- English-language bra-making content (Bra Sewists, Curvy Sewing Collective, etc.) clusters at D+ cup; even small-size content rarely goes below A cup.

## Evidence AGAINST (or Nuancing)

### Shared underlying theory
- **The same fit taxonomy applies.** Korean blogs freely cite Western categories: FOB/FOT, sister sizes, seam types (Cleo Juna for shallow vertical seams, Panache Envy for side panels). Rysso_ references Beverly Johnson's Pinup Girls Classic and Shelley patterns by name, as the standard Western reference.
- The underlying **mathematics of bra construction is shared** — darts, seam allowances, wire channels. What differs is size defaults, emphasis, and where the knowledge is written down.

### Japanese home sewing skews soft-bra
- Japanese hobbyist content overwhelmingly favours non-wired soft bras. Wired drafting at home is so scarce that the one documented Japanese home sewer doing it (ameblo weathersf) **imports a Western pattern** (Maya Bra from BraBuilders.com) rather than using a Japanese source.
- This skew is itself a default difference — but it's a market gap rather than a technical difference: Japanese professional/academic bra making (HEAVEN Japan, Bunka graduate work, armeria-marine) is fully wired. The gap is at the hobbyist level.

### Large-bust content exists in Asian communities too
- Rysso_ discusses Panache bras (large-bust brand) and Cleo (also large-cup range). The theory travels; the practice targets smaller sizes.

---

## Recurring Patterns (Synthesis)

1. **Fit anchored to underbust + explicit gore behavior + upper-edge openness** — the three functional variables that all Asian sources return to, regardless of language.

2. **Default size is smaller.** Documented drafting cases in Korean and Chinese technical sources cluster at underbust 65–75 / A–B. This is not "small by their standards" — it IS their standard.

3. **Gore height is a body-shape variable, not a style variable.** Chinese sources explicitly say so. Western sources treat it as aesthetics. This is a genuine assumption difference.

4. **Wire selection is the primitive, not the derived quantity.** Asian industry drafts cup from wire; Western hobbyist patterns draft body first, wire last.

5. **Published dimension data exists for small-size wire variants in Japan.** The geometry Jum needs (narrow-root, ~5.3cm wide, shorter arc) is sold retail in Japan. It doesn't appear in Western catalogs. This is a market difference with a practical consequence.

## What This Means Practically

For a small, shallow, narrow-framed person:

| Problem | Western approach | Asian approach (better) |
|---------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Cup too deep | Adjust pattern (complex) | Start with 2-part vertical seam cup; drape from body |
| Wire too wide | Not addressed in catalogs | Order L-type wire from jimura.jp |
| Gore floating | Not discussed; try smaller size | Explicit: use low gore (低鸡心) |
| Pattern too large | Grade down (loses proportions) | Draft from wire at actual size; Korean 70B block is a real starting block |
| Upper cup gaping | Go up a cup size (wrong solution) | Choose closed upper edge (컵 상변 닫힘) |
| No suitable retail bra | ThirdLove, Pepper (generic) | Wacoal ウンナナクール (japan proxy); Lulalu; Little Women UK |
