## Chinese Textbooks (Most Technical)

### 《内衣纸样设计原理与实例》 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Author:** 常建亮 (Chang Jianliang)
- **Publisher:** 上海科学技术出版社 (Shanghai Scientific & Technical Publishers)
- **Year:** 2007 | **Pages:** 226 | **ISBN:** 9787532389261
- **Douban:** https://book.douban.com/subject/2323220/
- **Contents:** 6 chapters — materials & construction basics; body aesthetics; top-type underwear; briefs; **bra design principles & examples** (ordinary bra / support bra / sports bra); bodysuits / swimwear / corsetry
- **Availability:** Out of print; used copies on Taobao and 孔夫子旧书网 (Kongfz)
- **Limitation:** Blocks use standard 70/75 A–B; must re-run formulas at 65 underbust

### 《内衣纸样设计原理与技巧》 ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Authors:** 印建荣 / 常建亮
- **Publisher:** 上海科学技术出版社
- **Year:** 2004 | **Pages:** 294 | **ISBN:** 9787532375967
- **Douban:** https://book.douban.com/subject/1173992/
- **Notes:** More drafting-heavy companion to the above; same series

## Japanese Books

### 『手作りランジェリー』 レディブティックシリーズ No.1404 ⭐⭐⭐
- **Publisher:** ブティック社 (Boutique-sha)
- **ISBN:** 4834714047 / 978-4834714040
- **Amazon.co.jp:** https://www.amazon.co.jp/dp/4834714047
- **Contents:** Bra + panties projects; some with 製図 (drafting), some pattern-less
- **Reviewer-noted technique:** "Cut open a worn-out bra and take the pattern from it" (着古した下着を切り刻んでパターンをとる) — self-referential sizing, automatically correct for the wearer's shape
- **Historical companion:** 別冊mc Sister DO!BOOK No.1 『手づくりランジェリー』(1985)
- **Small-frame note:** Japanese commercial patterns default to 65-band sizes — fits natively where they exist
- **Availability:** Amazon.co.jp (new/used); needs Japan proxy for international shipping

## Pattern Companies

### Pinup Girls Patterns (Beverly V. Johnson, Canada) ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **URL:** https://www.pinupgirlspatterns.com
- **Language:** English
- **Specialty:** Wired bra patterns; thorough fitting instructions; widely used globally
- **Referenced by:** Korean bra bloggers (rysso_); Japanese home sewers (weathersf ameblo); appears to be the de facto standard Western bra pattern for Asian home sewers lacking Japanese-language alternatives
- **Sizing method:** Bottom Cup Depth (BCD) — measure from apex seam to wire line; grade up/down to fit
- **Relevant patterns for small/shallow busts:**
  - **Sweet Sixteen:** Repeatedly recommended for shallow breasts; adjustable BCD
  - **Classic:** Diagonal seam; cited by rysso_ for diagonal-seam discussion
  - **Shelley:** Diagonal seam with side panel

### Pattern adjustment for shallow busts (Pinup Girls)
- Reduce BCD (Bottom Cup Depth) one step down without changing wire length
- "Pinch out" excess fabric from lower cup to flatten projection
- Walk seam lines after adjustment to ensure lengths still match
- Use muslin toile first; pin out excess on body to determine needed reduction
