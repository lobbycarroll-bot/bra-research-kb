# Underwire Geometry and Sourcing

Last materially reviewed: 2026-09-06

## Current conclusion

Country of manufacture is not a geometry. A wire sold in Japan or manufactured in China is not necessarily wider, flatter, shorter, or better suited to an Asian wearer. Select and compare wires by their physical curves and worn behavior.

For Jum, **Emerald Erin France size 36 remains the working baseline** because it has already fitted better than the other wires physically tested. Published Japanese candidates reviewed so far are considerably deeper and more U-shaped at similar arc lengths. They are useful comparative evidence, but they are not currently recommended purchases.

## Jum: current fit evidence

- Underbust: approximately 78.0 cm
- Full bust/apex circumference: approximately 81.5 cm
- Typical commercial size: US 34AA-34A
- Best-fitting commercial bra so far: Pepper size S, style BRA036, RN 174794
- Best-fitting loose underwire tested so far: Emerald Erin France size 36
- Wearer report: most other wires feel too U-shaped and too narrow

Working hypothesis: very low volume and projection with a broad/shallow root. Splayed or wide-set positioning may also be present, but breast-root width and breast spacing must be evaluated separately.

**Do not infer a wire number from the 78 cm underbust.** Garment band size, cup letter, and a supplier's wire number are different systems.

## Measurement vocabulary

For comparisons in this file:

- **Span:** straight tip-center to tip-center distance on the relaxed wire
- **Depth:** maximum perpendicular distance from the tip-to-tip line to the deepest point of the relaxed curve
- **Arc:** length measured along the wire centerline
- **Spring:** increase in span under a specified load or when installed in a tensioned band

Jimura's diagram uses **A = span, B = depth, C = arc length**. Earlier versions of this knowledge base incorrectly described A/B as height/width. Jimura does not publish separate center- and outer-tip heights in its A/B/C table.

## France 36 baseline

Official Emerald Erin facts:

- The France family is described as shallow and splayed, with relatively short center and underarm tips.
- Made in France from nickel-free, PET-coated 24-gauge flat steel.
- France size 36 official arc length: **172 mm**.
- A printable 1:1 curve is available from the supplier.

Provisional measurements estimated from the printable curve:

- Relaxed span: approximately **119 mm**
- Relaxed depth: approximately **36 mm**

The span and depth are estimates, not supplier-published specifications. Verify them on an untouched physical France 36 using a flatbed scan or calibrated photograph. Gauge, stiffness and load-versus-spring behavior remain to be measured.

## Jimura status and sample options

There is no evidence that Jimura as a shop is closing. The shop remains active and lists other current products. Its homepage currently warns of shipping delays related to the proprietor's health.

The **U/C/L wire families themselves are discontinued** and will end when remaining stock is exhausted. C75 is already unavailable.

The sample listing offers:

- `実物大コピーの全サイズ見本`: a mailed set of full-size paper copies, not a downloadable PDF
- Individual physical U, C or L all-size samples: one wire of each available size
- Combined physical U/C/L sample set: one of each available wire; C75 is replaced by its full-size paper copy

The paper copy preserves only the relaxed two-dimensional outline. It does not preserve cross-section, stiffness, tip coating, spring or worn behavior.

### Jimura purchase decision

Do not make Jimura the production basis for Jum's bra. None of the measured candidates is close enough to France 36 to justify an international order solely for fitting. A physical/paper sample set is optional archival research only.

## Contemporary Japanese retail component source

BraPartsShop currently offers Japanese-made L wires in sizes 4-12. It publishes span, depth and arc length and states a 2.0 x 0.6 mm cross-section for these sizes. The shop recommends no.4 for bodies below its chart, then reducing cup height rather than using a narrower wire.

That advice is conceptually relevant to broad-root/low-volume fitting, but the actual no.4 and no.5 curves are much deeper than France 36. They are not recommended immediate purchases for Jum.

Source: https://braparts.theshop.jp/items/135902443

## What the Chinese sources actually establish

The Chinese-language articles already archived in this project are valuable, but they do not document a retail family of Chinese wires with full-size curves.

### Consumer/body-shape knowledge

天衣E恋 describes `圆盘型` (plate/disc shape) as a **large breast base with low projection** and recommends a low gore (`低鸡心`) for small/flat busts (`贫乳`). Its companion article says wire geometry is derived from breast height, spacing and chest-wall curve.

- Translation: https://bra-research.netlify.app/articles/tianyi-elian-bra-components
- Original: https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/34795006

### Industrial drafting method

沉默-霸主 documents the Chinese wire-first workflow: select the wire, draft the cup and cradle from its curve, and make the cup-bottom length equal to wire arc plus approximately 1.5 cm of channel allowance.

- Translation: https://bra-research.netlify.app/articles/sina-bra-pattern-drafting-wire-first
- Original: https://blog.sina.com.cn/s/blog_4400165f010127m7.html

These sources support the **fit model and drafting method**. They do not prove that a generic Chinese wire will be wide/shallow, nor do they identify an off-the-shelf France 36 equivalent.

## Chinese manufacturing opportunity

China is more promising as an eventual manufacturing source than as a hobby-retail wire catalog. Component factories manufacture to the buyer's specification and often serve both domestic and export brands; manufacturing location therefore does not imply a domestic Chinese body-fit geometry.

### Appropriate China inquiry

Do not send only the provisional 119 x 36 x 172 mm estimate. Supply an untouched physical France 36 plus a calibrated 1:1 scan and request:

1. The closest existing stock curves, with full-scale drawings and span/depth/arc/tip heights.
2. Cross-section, material, coating and stiffness information.
3. Five-to-ten sample pairs before any production commitment.
4. An exact-copy sample plus controlled variants: slightly wider/flatter and slightly narrower/deeper.
5. Load-versus-span or recommended installed spring data.

If a factory requires an industrial MOQ before producing fit samples, it is not useful for the current research phase.

## Current purchase recommendation

1. **Emerald Erin France 36, five-pair option** for prototypes and finished garments.
2. **France 34/36/38 fitting pack** only if adjacent sizes have not already been tested in a properly tensioned cradle.
3. Matching underwire channeling.
4. Skip Jimura U/C/L and BraPartsShop L no.4/no.5 for the immediate build.

Preserve one unused France 36 as the master reference. Added visual bust volume should be developed in the cup while retaining the fitted root wire unless physical testing shows that the root itself should change.

## Repeatable fitting and measurement protocol

1. Trace the inframammary fold/root independently on each side of the body.
2. Compare the relaxed wire without pushing it into breast tissue.
3. Record center-tip and outer-tip position separately; do not collapse root width and breast spacing into one variable.
4. Install candidates in the same cradle and band so band tension is controlled.
5. Measure relaxed span and worn span to quantify spring and distortion.
6. Record whether each wire sits on tissue, extends beyond the root, tips outward, rotates or changes cup shape.
7. Photograph or scan every wire against a millimeter grid and label orientation, supplier, family and size.
8. Distinguish an unsprung curve match from a worn/tensioned match in every conclusion.

## Corrections to prior conclusions

The following earlier claims are withdrawn:

- That Western suppliers offer only one wire geometry. Emerald Erin alone offers multiple distinct families.
- That Japanese wires are intrinsically narrower-root or better suited to Asian bodies.
- That Jimura L65 is the best starting fit for Jum.
- That Jimura A/B/C dimensions mean height/width/length.
- That the measured Japanese L wires are promising France 36 equivalents.

The supported conclusion is narrower: **Jum appears to benefit from an unusually open, shallow and splayed wire, and France 36 is presently the only physically tested successful example.**
