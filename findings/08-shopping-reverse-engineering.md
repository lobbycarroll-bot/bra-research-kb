# Reverse-Engineering Shopping Guide — Current Fit Architecture

**Status:** 2026-09-06. Rebuilt from current body measurements and the best physical wire trace. Earlier recommendations for band 65 or 75 are retired.

## Current evidence

- Direct body measurements supplied in the parallel fitting work: **78.0 cm snug underbust** and **81.5 cm standing/apex bust**.
- Wacoal Japan defines band 80 as **77.5–82.5 cm**. Its nominal differences are AAA about 5 cm, AA about 7.5 cm, and A about 10 cm. The two measurements therefore point to **band 80**, with standing volume below even nominal AAA80. Do not convert the 3.5 cm difference mechanically into a confident retail cup without the remaining measurements and a try-on.
- Known wearable reference: Pepper size S, style BRA036, RN 174794. Its success matters, but its alpha grading and soft/moulded construction do not override the direct Japanese band chart.
- Best physical wire match reported so far: **Emerald Erin France wire, size 36**. `36` is the supplier's wire identifier, not a 36-inch band recommendation. The France curve is shallow, splayed, short at center and underarm, and intended especially for petite/small cups that find classic wires too narrow and tall.

## What the fit suggests

Jum's present problem is not simply "small cup." It is **very low volume distributed across a relatively broad, shallow root**. Cup volume and wire/root size must be decoupled. A bra can need a France 36-sized footprint while still needing less than a conventional AAA80 cup volume.

Build in two layers:

1. **Anchored base:** wire/cradle follows the root, band carries load, cup has very little projection and does not gape.
2. **Controlled silhouette:** removable cookies, graduated foam, side slings, vertical lift seams or dress cups add projection/cleavage only after the base is stable.

This supports natural everyday garments and deliberate augmentation without forcing the ribcage into a too-small band or using a narrow wire to fake a smaller cup.

## Revised Wacoal choices

### Best overall Wacoal try-on: BMM022 in AA-A80

Official page: https://store.wacoal.jp/disp/01_BMM022.html

Why it moves to first place:

- AA-A80 is a live selectable size.
- It is explicitly engineered to prevent the upper edge floating and to keep the bust apex covered.
- The lining is intended to sit close to a low-volume breast and make a natural, non-bulky line.
- It is wired, so it gives more useful root/cradle evidence than a generic bralette.
- Current reviews include a thin-upper-body wearer who says it fits naturally without thick padding and without wire pain.

Risk: AA-A is a pooled commercial size and Jum's standing difference is below nominal Wacoal AAA80. Treat this as a **returnable fit experiment**, not a measurement-derived guarantee.

### Closest labelled Wacoal size: My Size Collection BPP330 in AAA80

Official page: https://store.wacoal.jp/disp/01_BPP330.html

This made-after-order Japanese 4/5-cup bra is graded separately by cup volume and offers AAA80. It is the best Wacoal specimen for studying true size-specific low-volume construction, natural rounding and moderate side gathering. It costs ¥12,980 and takes roughly one to one-and-a-half months. Because Jum measures below nominal AAA80, buy it only after the body measurements are repeated and preferably after a cheaper AAA80/AA-A80 try-on.

### Best Liberty wearable experiment: JB3570 in L

Official page: https://store.wacoal.jp/disp/01_JB3570.html

Change the earlier recommendation from **S to L**. Wacoal's current mapping puts A80 inside L; S is only A65/B65 and is not compatible with a 78 cm underbust. JB3570 is the attractive Liberty choice and may tolerate low volume because its one-piece breathable Elc moulded cup and lift sheet are flexible.

Limits:

- L is a broad alpha grade covering A80 as well as several larger cup/band combinations. It is not a precise cup-volume test.
- It is a moulded wireless architecture, not the vertical/plate-seamed construction sought for pattern study.
- Buy only where L is in stock and the forwarding/retailer route preserves a practical return or exchange path. Current Wacoal color stock is variable.

### Liberty construction specimen, not a fit recommendation: JB2323 B75

Official page: https://store.wacoal.jp/disp/01_JB2323.html

This wired Liberty `さあ、わたし` bra has a wider wire, internal side support, high-side/back structure, removable pad and B-cup-specific thick rounded padding. It is useful to dissect, but B75 is much too much nominal volume for the current 78/81.5 measurements and its band is below Wacoal's interval. Buy only as an intentionally non-fitting construction control at a steep discount.

Retire the earlier B65 recommendations (JB2780, JB2400, JB3840, JB2310) as wearable candidates. Their cups start too large and their bands are dramatically too short. They remain optional teardown specimens only.

## Other Asian-market buys worth the money

### 1. Nissen Petitshu soft-wire bra, AAA80

Official product: https://www.nissen.co.jp/item/CFC0326A0002

This is the best inexpensive Japanese low-volume control. The range includes AAA80. It uses an L-shaped soft wire, crossover/cachcoeur front, thin removable urethane pad, powernet cup surface, no side bone and a folded back edge. The design explicitly rejects heavy padding while still allowing modest added volume. It is more useful than buying another conventional B65 Une Nana Cool model because it tests the correct band and the lowest readily available commercial cup.

### 2. SATAMI BR-05951, 36A

Official product: https://www.online-satami.com/BR-05951-BK-Black-Invisible-T-shirt-Bra-Cup-A-D

This Hong Kong design is the strongest non-Wacoal retail comparator for Jum's geometry. SATAMI explicitly changes the A-cup architecture to an **extra-wide underwire and low center gore for small-busted women with a wide breast base**. It adds medium graduated padding and has pockets for extra pads, plus crescent-shaped underarm panels and a firm double-layer back. Size 36A is currently listed. It directly demonstrates the desired two-layer system: broad-root anchoring plus controlled artificial volume.

### 3. Sabina Modern V wired bra, 36A if selectable

Collection: https://www.sabina.co.th/en/category/modern-v

This is the best Thai augmentation specimen. Sabina built its reputation in Thai small-bust push-up products. Modern V uses a V-shaped side pad to push tissue inward; the current Level 3 specification uses roughly 2.3–3.0 cm foam and is described as adding about half a cup. Buy one wired 36A, not multiple colors. Its research value is the **shape and placement of the padding**, not its label or its ability to prove root fit.

### 4. Comfortlab Dual Cool Volume V-cut, 90P (mapped to 80A)

Official product: https://comfortlab.co.kr/m/product.html?GfDT=bmp8W1w%3D&branduid=3357086&mcode=007&scode=&search=&xcode=006

This Korean wireless comparison uses a 14.5-degree bridge band and a 2 cm teardrop pad aimed at filling the upper chest; size 90P is mapped by the maker to 70C/75B/80A. It is useful after the first fit round to study upper-pole volume and a non-wire anchoring bridge. It is not a priority fit purchase because the pooled cup mapping may still be too voluminous.

### Deprioritize

- **Feast:** unusually thoughtful Japanese small-bust design language, but current wired products checked are centered on very small bands (for example AA60/A60/B60), while several wireless styles top out around 72 cm underbust. Do not force Jum into them merely because the brand markets to small busts.
- Generic marketplace `80AA` bras from China/Taiwan/Vietnam: many are thick moulded cups with weak technical disclosure. Buy only when a listing provides wire width/height, pad-thickness map, internal photos and a return route.
- Korean 80A bras that publish only cup height: potentially useful later, but lower value than SATAMI's explicit wide-base A-cup geometry or Nissen's true AAA80 option.

## Home-construction purchases

### Highest-value first build

1. **Five pairs of Emerald Erin France wires in size 36** so fitting bands and finished samples can be compared without cannibalizing a bra: https://shop.emeralderin.com/products/france-underwires
2. **Hari Ito Mésange PDF pattern**, the current pattern explicitly matched by Emerald Erin to the France wire. It covers 28AA–48H and provides a one-piece darted stretch-lace cup plus full-band and longline options: https://hariitolingerie.com/
3. Materials for three controlled versions of the same cradle: stable sheer cup lining; stretch lace; 2–3 mm cut-and-sew foam; powernet in two strengths; channeling; band/neckline elastic; removable teardrop pads; hook-and-eye; and plastic boning for the longline test.

Make the first three cups on the same France-36 cradle:

- unlined or lightly lined shallow dart cup (baseline),
- cut-and-sew foam cup with an offset/vertical lift seam,
- the same cup with a removable lower-outer cookie or side sling (augmentation).

### Second pattern for silhouette control

The Pin-Up Girls Ruby or Sapphire is useful after the France-wire cradle is stable. Both use an offset vertical seam and shortened upper cup for a rounder, lifted/push-up silhouette; both can use cut-and-sew foam. They are not direct France-wire matches, so adapt the wire line rather than assuming the commercial cradle fits.

- Ruby full band: https://www.braandcorsetsupplies.com/product/ruby-full-band-bra-pattern/
- Sapphire partial band: https://www.braandcorsetsupplies.com/product/sapphire-partial-band-bra-pattern/

## What the regional research actually supports

The useful evidence is not a claim that every Asian body or brand shares one shape. It is that low-volume customers are visible enough in several Asian markets to produce design rules that English-language mass retail often omits:

- Japan offers retail AAA80 and pooled AA-A80.
- SATAMI specifies a different A-cup wire architecture: extra-wide and low-gore for a broad, small-volume base.
- Thai Sabina treats small-bust augmentation as a core product system, with multiple documented foam levels and vector-specific pads.
- Korean products commonly publish garment measurements and map alpha/pooled cups back to band-cup equivalents; Comfortlab also documents pad geometry and bridge angle.
- Across these markets, the dominant commercial answer is still often moulded foam. That makes the garments excellent padding, side-sling and silhouette references, but not automatically good patterns for a seamed custom cup.

The strongest home-sewing path is therefore hybrid: use the **France 36 root trace as the non-negotiable base**, test an Asian AAA80/AA-A80 wearable control, and selectively copy the most useful Asian padding and lift mechanisms onto a wire-first custom cradle.

## Measurement record for every purchase

Before cutting or washing, record relaxed and stretched band; wire trace and spring; wire tip heights; gore width/height; cup seam line or moulded-shell perimeter; horizontal and vertical cup depth; apex location; pad thickness on a 2 cm grid; pad-pocket size; side-sling shape; strap attachment vector; elastic tension; layer order; and front/side/three-quarter photos on the same body or cast. Then repeat with no pad, included pad and experimental pad.
