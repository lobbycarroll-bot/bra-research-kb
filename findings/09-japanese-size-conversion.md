## The essential rule

Cup letters are category names within each sizing system; they are **not directly interchangeable between countries or brands**. The Japanese number is the nominal underbust in centimeters, while Japanese cup steps are defined in 2.5 cm increments of top-bust minus underbust.

### Wacoal Japan definitions

| Japanese cup | Nominal top-minus-underbust difference |
|---|---:|
| AAA | about 5 cm |
| AA | about 7.5 cm |
| A | about 10 cm |
| B | about 12.5 cm |
| C | about 15 cm |

Wacoal places a 78.0 cm underbust in its **80 band** range (77.5–82.5 cm). Its nominal AAA80 body measurements are about 80 cm underbust and 85 cm top bust. Jum's measured 81.5 cm top is below even that nominal interval, so the chart does not yield a supported cup letter from this measurement pair.

Official Wacoal chart: https://www.wacoal.jp/advice/contents/post-15.html

## Alpha-size warning

Do not equate an S across brands. Pepper S groups its US 34AA/34A/34B sizes. The renewed Une Nana Cool 364 JB3570 uses its own mapping: S=A65/B65, M includes A75, and L includes B75. Identical alpha labels can describe very different bodies and cup volumes.

## Measurements that override conversion tables

Record in centimeters:

1. snug underbust, level and measured after exhaling normally
2. standing full bust
3. leaning full bust if practical
4. current Pepper band's relaxed and stretched lengths
5. cup gaping, wire position and band movement in the Pepper bra

Use the conversion chart only to choose initial try-on sizes. The correct Japanese size is the one whose band, wire and cup geometry work on Jum.
